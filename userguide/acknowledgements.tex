\chapter{Acknowledgements}

\homot was inspired by, and heavily based on, the excellent work that our collaborators at the European Bioinformatics Institute did on the graphical front-end for the HMMER web server \url{https://www.ebi.ac.uk/Tools/hmmer/home}.  Seeing how much our colleagues benefited from a graphical exploration of search results motivated us to develop a stand-alone tool that provides the same benefits for searches run on other systems.

We are indebted to the members and alumni of the Eddy and Rivas labs who provided feedback on earlier versions of \homot.  \homot was funded in part by the National Institute of health under grant R01HG009116.  Any opinions or conclusions expressed in this document are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the viewpoint of the {NIH}.

\homot relies on the taxonomic trees created by the National Center for Biotechnology Information \url{https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/}, the genomic families identified by Pfam \url{http://pfam.xfam.org/}, and the sequence sets created by \uniprot \url{https://www.uniprot.org/}, and we thank the members of these organizations for their work.  We would also like to thank the other developers of the \hmmer software package for their efforts.

\homot was implemented using the Rust \url{www.rustup.rs} and Dart \url{dart.dev} programming languages,  and the Flutter \url{flutter.dev} GUI toolkit. We would like to thank everyone who has contributed to these tools and to making them available for public use.
